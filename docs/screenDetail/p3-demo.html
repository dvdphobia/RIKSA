What you're looking at

  • Left = P3.S1 Gamification Profile. Private dashboard. Header says "Your progress". The 2×2 grid shows 4 stats about you only: Total XP, Current Streak, Highest Streak (21), Lessons Done. Below it: tabs for XP ledger, achievements, rank history.

  • The 4th cell is the tell. P3.S1 shows Highest Streak (depth about yourself). P3.S6 shows Province Rank (social standing others care about). Same first 3 stats, different 4th = different purpose.
