Frequently asked questions

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Studying

What is RIKSA for?

RIKSA helps Grade 12 students prepare for the Bac II exam. Each subject has a roadmap of short lessons: you read, write a reflection in your own words, and try a few practice questions. The roadmap always shows where you started, where you are and what's left.

Which subjects are there?

Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science, following the Grade 12 syllabus. More lessons are added over time.

Can I use RIKSA without an account?

Yes. As a guest you can browse subjects and try lessons. Create an account to save your progress, earn XP, keep a streak and join the community.

Why do lessons ask me to write a reflection?

Explaining an idea in your own words is one of the best ways to remember it. There is no single right answer. Write what you understood and what is still unclear.

XP, streaks and your buddy

How do I earn XP?

Finishing lessons, completing daily and weekly quests, opening the weekly chest and unlocking achievements all give XP. Your rank goes up as your total XP grows.

How does a streak work?

Finish at least one lesson on a day (Cambodia time) to keep your streak going. Miss a day and it starts again.

What is my buddy, and how do I get hats and glasses?

Your buddy is your study companion. Spend XP in the Market to buy items, then choose what it wears in the Wardrobe. Spending XP doesn't lower your rank.

Community

Why is my post or article "in review"?

Everything posted is checked automatically. Most posts appear straight away. A few are held until a moderator looks at them, usually because a word could be read the wrong way. You'll get a notification when a held article is reviewed.

Why was my post or comment blocked?

It probably contained language or content that breaks the Community Agreement. Try rewriting it. If you think it was a mistake, contact us.

How do I report something?

Open the menu on the post, comment, article or resource and choose Report. Moderators review every report, and the person you report isn't told who sent it.

What shows up in Notifications?

Reactions and comments on your posts, replies to your comments, likes on your articles, and a moderator's decision on an article that was held for review.

AI tutor

Is the AI tutor always right?

No. It is helpful for explanations and practice, but it can make mistakes. Check important facts with your lessons or teacher.

Is there a limit on AI messages?

There may be a daily limit so that everyone gets a fair share. If you reach it, you can ask again after midnight, Cambodia time.

Account

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, choose "Forgot password" and follow the steps sent to your email. If you signed up with Google or Telegram, sign in with that instead.

My account is locked.

After several wrong passwords, an account is locked for a short time to protect it. Wait a few minutes and try again.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Contact us at [contact email to be added] from the email linked to your account, and we will delete it. See the Privacy Policy for what that removes.
