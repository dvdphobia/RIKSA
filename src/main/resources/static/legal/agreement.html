Community Agreement

Last updated: 30 September 2026

RIKSA's community is a place for students to help each other learn. When you post, comment, react, write an article or add a resource, you agree to these rules.

Do

  • Be kind. Everyone here is studying for the same exam. Explain, encourage and disagree politely.
  • Keep it about learning. Questions, explanations, study tips, notes, progress and school life are all welcome.
  • Share what's yours. Post your own work and photos, and say where something came from when you share it.
  • Report problems. Use Report on anything that breaks these rules. Reports are private.

Don't

  • Bully, threaten, insult or embarrass anyone, or encourage others to.
  • Post hate against people for their ethnicity, religion, gender, disability, background or anything else about who they are.
  • Post sexual content, nudity, or anything that sexualises anyone. Content involving a minor is removed and may be reported to the authorities.
  • Post violence, self-harm encouragement, drugs, weapons or other dangerous content.
  • Share other people's private information: phone numbers, addresses, photos of them without permission, or their accounts.
  • Share leaked exam papers or help others cheat in exams.
  • Spam, advertise, or post links to unsafe or unrelated websites.
  • Pretend to be someone else, including teachers or RIKSA staff.

How moderation works

  • Posts, comments and articles are checked automatically when you send them. Clearly harmful content is blocked. Content that might break the rules is held until a moderator reviews it.
  • Moderators review reports and held content. They can approve, hide or remove it, and remove photos.
  • Breaking the rules can lead to a warning, content removal, a temporary suspension, or losing your account. Serious cases skip the warning.
  • If you think a decision was wrong, contact us and a person will look at it again.
