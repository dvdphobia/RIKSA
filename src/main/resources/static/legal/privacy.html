Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

RIKSA is a study app for Grade 12 students in Cambodia preparing for the Bac II exam. This policy explains what information RIKSA collects, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

RIKSA does not sell your information, does not show advertising, and does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your username, display name, email address (if you sign up with email) and profile picture.
  • If you sign in with Google or Telegram, the account identifier and basic profile details those services share with us. We never see your Google or Telegram password.
  • Your province, preferred language and the study buddy you choose.

Your studying

  • Lessons you open and finish, your answers to practice questions, the reflections you write, and saved lessons.
  • XP, streaks, quests, rank and achievements, and items you get for your buddy.

What you share with others

  • Community posts, comments, photos, reactions, blog articles, and links you add to lessons.
  • Reports you send about other people's content.

AI tutor conversations

  • Messages you send to the AI tutor and its replies, so you can come back to a conversation.

Security information

  • Sign-in attempts, used to lock an account temporarily after repeated wrong passwords, and records of actions taken by moderators.

How we use it

  • To run the app: keep you signed in, save your progress, and show your lessons, feed and profile.
  • To show your progress to you, and your username, rank and XP on leaderboards.
  • To keep the community safe: posts, comments and articles are checked automatically for abusive or unsafe content, and some are held for a moderator to review.
  • To tell you about activity on your content, such as reactions, comments and replies.
  • To answer AI tutor questions (see below).

Who can see what

  • Other students can see your public profile (username, display name, picture, province, rank and achievements), your community posts and comments, and published blog articles.
  • Moderators and administrators can see reported and held content and account details they need to handle reports, fix problems or keep the community safe.
  • The AI provider. To answer the AI tutor, the text of your message, recent messages in that conversation and the lesson you are studying are sent to our AI provider, OpenRouter, which routes it to an AI model. Don't put personal details you wouldn't want shared into AI tutor messages.
  • Sign-in providers. If you use Google or Telegram to sign in, those services know you used them to sign in to RIKSA.

We don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Notifications on your phone

Study reminders are scheduled on your own device. You can turn them off in the app's settings or in your phone's settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. Content you delete is removed from the app. When an account is deleted, its profile, progress and private data are deleted, and community content is removed or anonymised.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile, username and picture at any time in the app.
  • Delete your own posts, comments and articles.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account, using the contact details below.

Students under 18

Most RIKSA users are secondary school students. If you are under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian knows you use RIKSA and has read this policy with you.

Security

Passwords are stored in a scrambled form that can't be read back, connections to our servers are encrypted, and your sign-in is kept in your phone's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
